Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

What this covers

This policy covers REMEDIE Engine, the tool that uploads videos to the REMEDIE YouTube channel. The tool is used by one person, the channel's owner, and only for that channel. Nobody else signs in to it.

What it uses from Google

REMEDIE Engine uses the YouTube API Services. When the channel owner signs in, Google asks for permission to upload videos. The tool uses that permission to upload the channel's own videos, set their title, description and thumbnail, and set whether they are private, unlisted or public.

It does not read your comments, messages, subscriptions, watch history, contacts or any other account data. It collects nothing from viewers of the channel.

What is stored, and where

The sign in token Google gives the tool is stored on the channel owner's own computers and nowhere else. It is not sold, shared or sent to anyone. The videos the tool uploads are made by the channel owner before upload, so the tool keeps no copy of anything it gets from YouTube.

How to remove access

Access can be removed at any time on the Google security page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once it is removed, the tool can no longer upload, and the stored token stops working.

YouTube and Google

By using a YouTube feature through this tool, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. How Google handles data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
